DPM rankings

DPM is our EuroLeague version of DARKO, the Daily Plus-Minus model created by Kostya Medvedovsky. It is a forward-looking rating: how many points per 100 possessions a player is expected to add over an average EuroLeague player. Box-score production and lineup results from every EuroLeague game since 2007-08 feed into it, and recent games count most.
