About our numbers

CourtLedger is built around two player rankings: AP (Added Points) first and DPM (DARKO) second. Everything else on the site (player dashboards, stats and scatter plots, trends, head-to-head, teams, game logs, lineups) is under More in the menu.

AP (Added Points)

AP (Added Points) is our Dean Oliver-inspired measure of how many points a player added to, or cost, his team's final margin in a single game. It is not plus-minus or net rating: it credits each player for what he actually produced, not just for being on the floor.

  • O-AP (offense) builds on Dean Oliver's individual offensive work: how much a player creates, how efficiently, and how often he uses possessions.
  • D-AP (defense) combines team defense, a player's own defensive stops, and tracked individual defense, meaning the points a player actually allowed on the possessions he defended. The more possessions he was tracked on, the more that tracking counts.
  • It always adds up. In every game, the players' Added Points sum exactly to the final score margin.

The AP ranking shows totals, per game, per 40 minutes, and per 100 team possessions on court. AP for a game is published once individual defensive data for that game is loaded. provisional defense marks any game where it isn't, so defense there is shared by playing time only.

DPM (DARKO)

DPM (DARKO) is our EuroLeague version of DARKO, the Daily Plus-Minus model created by Kostya Medvedovsky (darko.app) for the NBA. It isn't a model we invented; we apply the DARKO approach to EuroLeague data. It is a forward-looking rating: how many points per 100 possessions a player is expected to add over an average EuroLeague player in his next game. It weighs every EuroLeague game since 2007-08, with recent games counting most, and blends box-score production with lineup results. O-DPM and D-DPM split it into offense and defense.

DPM is a current rating. In the DPM ranking, the season switch picks which season's players are listed and their games and minutes; the rating shown is always the latest one.

Freshness

Both rankings are rebuilt after every EuroLeague game day.

DPM ratings are current through the latest game in the DPM column. AP appears for a game once its defensive data is loaded, so it can trail DPM by a few games.

Changelog

30 Oct 2026. AP was recalculated for 2025-26 and 2026-27 with a refined Dean Oliver-based method. Offense and defense now follow Oliver's box-score framework more closely, and tracked individual defense counts more for players tracked on more possessions. Every game still adds up exactly to the final margin. AP values from before this date are not comparable.
