About our numbers

Added Points (AP)

Added Points (AP) is our Dean Oliver-inspired measure of how many points a player added to, or cost, his team's final margin in a single game. It is not plus-minus or net rating: it credits each player for what he actually produced, not just for being on the floor.

  • Offense builds on Dean Oliver's individual offensive work: how much a player creates, how efficiently, and how often he uses possessions.
  • Defense combines team defense with tracked individual defense, meaning the points a player actually allowed on the possessions he defended.
  • It always adds up. In every game, the players' Added Points sum exactly to the final score margin.

Values are shown per game, in totals, and per 100 team possessions on court. provisional defense marks games where individual defensive data isn't loaded yet, so defense is shared by playing time only.

DPM

DPM is our EuroLeague version of DARKO, the Daily Plus-Minus model created by Kostya Medvedovsky for the NBA. It isn't a model we invented; we apply the DARKO approach to EuroLeague data. It is a forward-looking rating: how many points per 100 possessions a player is expected to add over an average EuroLeague player in his next game. It weighs every EuroLeague game since 2007-08, with recent games counting most, and blends box-score production with lineup results.
